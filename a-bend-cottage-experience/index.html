Local and personal

More than just vacation homes

Our individualized cottages are the perfect way to make your Bend stay a memorable one. Every home is in the historic downtown, the trendy west side, or the Old Mill District right on the river, so you can park the car and live like a local.

Whether it’s a couples’ getaway, family fun, relocation, medical stays, or business travel, there’s a home and a plan to fit. Nightly, weekly, and monthly stays are all available, and The Wild Pines is set aside for stays of 30 nights or more.

Walk from the cottages

Drake Park & Mirror Pond

Riverside lawns and paths along the Deschutes, between downtown and the west side.

Historic Downtown Bend

Shops, theaters, galleries, coffee, and McMenamins Old St. Francis.

Galveston Avenue District

Restaurants, breweries like 10 Barrel, and The Lot food truck court.

Up the hill

Mt. Bachelor

Skiing and snowboarding in winter, scenic lift rides in summer.

Tumalo Falls

A short hike to one of Central Oregon’s best-known waterfalls.

Phil’s Trail network

Endless mountain bike trails just west of town.
