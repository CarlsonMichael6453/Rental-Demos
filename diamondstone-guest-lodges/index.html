A hidden treasure in La Pine

DiamondStone is a private mini resort on forested acreage in La Pine, with warm, comfy suites, an event space, and activities all year.

Guests rent a single suite or the whole Homestead Lodge, which works for couples, families, groups, and business travelers. The Little Deschutes River has public access nearby.

Newberry Country

Explore around La Pine

Real places in every direction from the lodge.

Volcano country

Newberry National Volcanic Monument

Paulina and East lakes, the Big Obsidian Flow, and Paulina Falls.

La Pine State Park

Riverside trails and Oregon’s “Big Tree” ponderosa.

Lava River Cave

A mile-long lava tube (seasonal).

Water

Little Deschutes River

Public access near the lodge.

Wickiup Reservoir

Boating and fishing to the west.

Cascade Lakes

Alpine lakes for summer paddling.

Day trips

Mt. Bachelor

Skiing and summer lift rides.

Crater Lake National Park

Oregon’s deep blue lake, a scenic drive south.

Sunriver

The Village, bike paths, and SHARC to the north.
