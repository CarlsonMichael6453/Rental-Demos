About the guest house

A private, beautifully appointed two-bedroom, two-bath guest house on 20-acre Sweetums Ranch, with views of the Three Sisters and Broken Top, horses, wildflowers, and lots of wildlife.

It’s about 8 miles to downtown Bend, 12 to Sisters, and 45 minutes to Mt. Bachelor. The ranch sits on the Oregon scenic bike route, with a big private yard, a hot tub, a seasonal pool, a tennis court, a hammock, and cornhole.
