Stay on the ranch

Alpaca Country Estates began with six Huacaya alpacas in 2006 and now raises a herd of more than 1,000 on 134 acres of Central Oregon pasture with views of the Cascades.

Guests can watch the babies play, get an alpaca nose kiss, picnic in the park-like grounds, or just stroll the meadows and look at the stars. Stay details (lodging type, sleeping, amenities) are placeholders to confirm with the ranch.
