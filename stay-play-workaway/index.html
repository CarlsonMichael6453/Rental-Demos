Stay · Play · Work

Your Central Oregon basecamp

A relaxing one-level home in Redmond, a short drive from Smith Rock State Park and other public lands with superb rock climbing and gorgeous trails. Skiing, snowboarding, and snowshoeing are within an easy drive, and after a day out, Redmond’s brewpubs, food carts, and restaurants are just minutes away.

Inside you’ll find a stocked gourmet kitchen, workspaces with high-speed internet, king beds, a gas fireplace with a big streaming TV, patio furniture with a grill, and laundry.

Redmond & the high desert

Explore Redmond

Real, currently open places for climbing days, event weekends, and nights out.

Climb, hike & ride

Smith Rock State Park

World-class climbing and canyon trails along the Crooked River, a quick drive north.

Dry Canyon Trail

Redmond’s paved trail through a canyon in the middle of town, good for an easy run or ride.

Gray Butte trails

Quieter high-desert trails for hiking, running, and biking past Smith Rock.

Cline Falls State Park

A riverside park with a waterfall hike west of town.

Redmond Caves

Free lava-tube caves in town. Bring a flashlight.

Eat & drink downtown

Wild Ride Brewing

Downtown’s landmark brewery, with food carts and fire pits on the patio.

Initiative Brewing

The brewery’s original Redmond pub, serving scratch-made pub food.

General Duffy’s Waterhole

A food cart pod and taphouse a few blocks from Wild Ride.

Redmond Burger Company

Hand-pressed burgers and hand-cut fries.

Grace and Hammer Pizzeria

Creative pizzas in a former church.

Events & easy logistics

Deschutes County Fair & Expo Center

Fairs, shows, and events all year, an easy drive from the house.

Redmond Airport (RDM)

Central Oregon’s airport, close by for flights and rental cars.

Redmond Cascade Swim Center

Indoor rec swim and lap swim for a rest day.

Mt. Bachelor & Hoodoo

Downhill and cross-country skiing and tubing within a scenic drive.
