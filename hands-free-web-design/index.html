Common questions

FAQ

Do I own the site?

Yes. I build it, and you own it. The domain is in your name, and you're not locked into a platform you don't control.

What does it cost each year?

Hosting is free. Your only yearly cost is the domain name, usually about $10 to $25, depending on where you buy it. The $60 a month care plan is optional, and so is the social and content add-on.

What if I already have a site on GoDaddy or Wix?

I can rebuild it so you own the site and pay less to keep it online. We can time the switch around your current renewal, so you aren't paying twice. Booking, phone numbers, and the links you already use can stay the same.

How fast can it be ready?

I start with a free demo so you can see the design first. Once you approve it and share any photos or details you want included, we can get the site live quickly. I'll give you a clear timeline when we talk.

Do I need to do anything?

As little as you want. I can start from what's already public about your business. If you have photos, a logo, or wording you like, I'll use those. After launch, the care plan covers updates, rate and menu changes, photos, and specials, so you don't have to manage the site yourself.
