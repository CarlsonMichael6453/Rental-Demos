Life at the Refugio

Peacocks, sunsets, and starry skies

Colorful wild peacocks wander the property as a symbol of good luck. Keep an eye out for Fabio, the rare white peacock.

The sun sets over the Cascades and rises to the east, and the open desert sky makes for incredible stargazing. The hosts’ family lives on the property, so quiet hours are respected on both sides.

Artful and roomy

About the home

Thoughtfully designed for both connection and privacy, the home has three bedrooms in total (two queens and one king), two separate living areas, and artistic touches throughout.

Outside, native perennials, fruit trees, and herbs grow around the property, all on a fully fenced, gated, and secure five acres just minutes from Smith Rock State Park.
