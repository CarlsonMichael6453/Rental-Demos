Sunriver & beyond

Explore Sunriver

Real places a short bike ride or drive from the lodges.

In Sunriver

SHARC

Sunriver’s aquatic and recreation center: indoor and outdoor pools, waterslides, a lazy river, and a winter sledding hill.

The Village at Sunriver

Shops, cafés, ice skating in winter, and summer events in the center of town.

Sunriver Nature Center & Observatory

Live raptors, a botanical garden, and night-sky viewing.

Paved bike paths

More than 30 miles of paths connecting the whole community.

Up the road

Mt. Bachelor

Skiing and snowboarding in winter, scenic lift rides in summer.

Benham Falls

A short forest walk to a big Deschutes River waterfall, just north of Sunriver.

Lava Lands & Lava Butte

The volcanic visitor center and a road to the top of a cinder cone.

Cascade Lakes Scenic Byway

Alpine lakes for paddling, fishing, and picnics in summer.

Day trips

High Desert Museum

Live animals and the story of the high desert, between Sunriver and Bend.

Downtown Bend & the Old Mill

Restaurants, breweries, and riverside shopping about 20 miles north.

Lava River Cave

A mile-long lava tube in Newberry National Volcanic Monument (seasonal).

Newberry Caldera

Paulina and East lakes, the Big Obsidian Flow, and Paulina Falls.
