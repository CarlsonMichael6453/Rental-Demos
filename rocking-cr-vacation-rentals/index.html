Around Tumalo & Bend

Real places a short drive from both homes.

Close to home

Tumalo State Park

A riverside park on the Deschutes with swimming, picnics, and trails.

Tumalo Falls

A 97-foot waterfall up Skyliner Road west of Bend (seasonal road access).

Cascade Lakes Scenic Byway

Alpine lakes for paddling and picnics in summer.

Bend & Sisters

Downtown Bend

Restaurants, breweries, and shops along the Deschutes.

Old Mill District

Riverside shopping, dining, and the Hayden Homes Amphitheater.

Downtown Sisters

Western storefronts, galleries, and cafés up Highway 20.

Seasons

Mt. Bachelor

Skiing and snowboarding in winter, scenic lift rides in summer.

Smith Rock State Park

Canyon hiking and world-class climbing north of Redmond.

High Desert Museum

Live animals and the story of the high desert, south of Bend.
