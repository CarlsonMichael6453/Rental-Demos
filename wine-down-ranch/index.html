Leave the hustle behind

About the ranch

A beautiful Central Oregon cattle ranch where the pace slows down. Kick back with a good book and a glass of wine, breathe in the ponderosa pines, and enjoy ranch living.

Choose the roomy Bunkhouse for up to five, or the Tiny Pine Cabin for two. Horse boarding and corrals are available, and the ranch also hosts weddings and events.
