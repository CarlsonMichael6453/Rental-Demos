Steps from the water

Explore downtown Bend

Real places you can walk, bike, or drive to from Mirror Pond.

Walkable

Drake Park

Right across the pond: lawns, shade, and summer events.

Downtown Bend

Wall and Bond Streets are full of restaurants, bars, and shops.

Tower Theatre

Bend’s restored 1940 theatre for concerts and films.

On the river

Deschutes River Trail

Miles of riverside trail through town.

Bend Whitewater Park

Surf waves and a float channel near the Old Mill.

Old Mill District

Riverside shopping, dining, and summer concerts.

Further out

Mt. Bachelor

Skiing in winter, scenic lift rides in summer.

Pilot Butte

A cinder cone in town with a 360-degree view.

Smith Rock State Park

Canyon hiking and climbing about 40 minutes north.
