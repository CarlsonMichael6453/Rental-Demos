Since 1965

A family cabin with history

The original log cabin was built in 1965 by Bend’s Miller family (of Miller Lumber) and has since been added onto and remodeled. Today it’s the Garner family’s riverfront getaway, surrounded by the Deschutes National Forest.

There are two bedrooms with queen beds, one and a half baths, and a futon for an extra guest. French doors open off each bedroom, and newly stained decks with built-in benches lead to the dock.
