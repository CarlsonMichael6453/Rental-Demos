About the chalet

A cabin-style retreat on a large treed lot that backs onto common space in Sunriver’s quiet south end. The owners have kept it as their own vacation home and a rental for three decades, with consistently high Vrbo ratings since 2013.

Inside: two bedrooms plus a landing with a futon, two bathrooms, a wood stove, Wi-Fi, smart TVs, a fully equipped kitchen, and a washer and dryer. Outside: a large sun deck with the new hot tub.

Sunriver & beyond

Explore from the chalet

Start at Harper’s Bridge and the bike path, then head out across the high desert.

Steps away

Deschutes River & Harper’s Bridge

A boat launch two blocks west, good for paddling and floating.

Sunriver bike paths

More than 30 miles of paved paths from the chalet.

Sunriver Lodge

Under a mile away, with dining and views of the meadow.

In Sunriver

SHARC

Sunriver’s aquatic and recreation center: indoor and outdoor pools, waterslides, a lazy river, and a winter sledding hill.

The Village at Sunriver

Shops, cafés, ice skating in winter, and summer events in the center of town.

Sunriver Nature Center & Observatory

Live raptors, a botanical garden, and night-sky viewing.

Paved bike paths

More than 30 miles of paths connecting the whole community.

Up the road

Mt. Bachelor

Skiing and snowboarding in winter, scenic lift rides in summer.

Benham Falls

A short forest walk to a big Deschutes River waterfall, just north of Sunriver.

Lava Lands & Lava Butte

The volcanic visitor center and a road to the top of a cinder cone.

Cascade Lakes Scenic Byway

Alpine lakes for paddling, fishing, and picnics in summer.
