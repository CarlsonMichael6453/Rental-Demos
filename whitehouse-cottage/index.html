About the cottage

A welcoming bungalow with a covered front porch that overlooks the Discovery Park area and the Cascade mountains. Restaurants, shopping, schools, medical care, and trails are all close by, and the home has upscale home-automation tech throughout.

The dining room seats a small group comfortably, with room for a few more when friends drop by. The small side yard is just right for the BBQ and the Jacuzzi®.

Your neighborhood

Explore NorthWest Crossing & Bend

Real, currently open places, starting with what’s a short walk from the porch.

Steps from the cottage

The Sparrow Bakery Northwest

The NWX outpost of Bend’s favorite bakery. Get an Ocean Roll early, before they sell out.

Thump Coffee at The Grove

House-roasted coffee inside The Grove Market Hall, with other food vendors right around you.

Bend Pizza Kitchen

Thin New York-style or Chicago deep dish, right on NW Crossing Drive.

Roundabout Books & Café

NorthWest Crossing’s independent bookshop, with a café inside for a slow morning.

Parks & trails

Discovery Park

The neighborhood park and lake the porch looks toward, good for a morning walk or a picnic.

NWX Farmers Market

Saturday mornings on NW Crossing Drive through the summer season.

Shevlin Park

A short drive west: forested trails along Tumalo Creek for hiking, running, and biking.

Tumalo Falls

A classic waterfall viewpoint west of town, with longer forest trails beyond.

Mt. Bachelor

Skiing in winter, and hiking and biking once the snow melts.

Into town

10 Barrel Brewing West Side

The original Galveston Avenue pub, known for pizza, a patio, and house taps.

Deschutes Brewery Public House

The downtown pub where Bend’s brewing story started.

Crux Fermentation Project

A tasting room with Cascade views and a big lawn for sunset pints.

Old Mill District

Shops, restaurants, and river paths, plus summer concerts at the amphitheater.
