About the cabin

Nestled in the Tollgate neighborhood just minutes from downtown Sisters, the home has vaulted, open-beam wood ceilings and a wall of windows framing the ponderosa pines.

The main floor has a king bedroom with sliding doors to the backyard and hot tub. Upstairs, a loft queen suite has its own bath and entrance, and the private Casita gives a third bedroom and bath.
