About the home

A single-story home in the beautiful Eagle Crest Resort. Of the resort’s pools and fitness centers, it’s closest to Lakeside Sports Center, with an infinity pool, hot tub, children’s pool, and the kids’ favorite spray park, plus a fitness center, an outdoor basketball court, tennis, and pickleball.

The primary bedroom has a king bed and an en-suite bathroom with a wonderful soaking tub. The guest bedrooms have queen beds, and one adds a single.

Eagle Crest & beyond

Explore Eagle Crest & Redmond

Real, currently open places, starting inside the resort and fanning out across Central Oregon.

At the resort

Lakeside Sports Center

The closest rec center: infinity pool, hot tub, kids’ pool, spray park, fitness center, and courts.

Eagle Crest Golf

Ridge and Resort championship courses, the Challenge course, and a putting course. The Ridge stays open into winter.

Deschutes River Trail

A riverside trail right at Eagle Crest, good for a walk or an easy ride.

Cline Falls State Park

A riverside park with a waterfall hike, just down the road from the resort.

Downtown Redmond

Wild Ride Brewing

A downtown landmark with food carts and fire pits on a big patio.

Initiative Brewing

Where the brewery started, with an allergen-friendly menu of scratch-made pub food.

Cascade Lakes Pub

From Redmond’s oldest brewery: burgers, flatbreads, and house beers.

Brickhouse

An upscale steakhouse and wine bar for a special dinner.

Diego’s Spirited Kitchen

Inventive Mexican and global dishes, with a back patio heated in winter.

Day trips

Smith Rock State Park

World-famous climbing and canyon hiking, an easy drive north.

Redmond Caves

Lava-tube caves right in town. Bring a flashlight.

Sisters

A western-style small town with shops, cafés, and mountain views, up the road from Eagle Crest.

Mt. Bachelor & Hoodoo

Two ski areas for skiing, tubing, and Hoodoo’s night skiing.

High Desert Museum

Live owls, otters, and more, plus the story of the high desert, south of Bend.
