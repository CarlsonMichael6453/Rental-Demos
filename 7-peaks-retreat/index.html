About 7 Peaks Retreat

Seven Peaks is a unique place to stay just minutes from Lake Billy Chinook, with a main house, two cabins, and a separate bath house, plus plenty of space for two RVs and boats.

Bring your own linens and towels, and the rest is set: a fully stocked kitchen, air-conditioning, and a wrap-around porch with a BBQ for long summer evenings after a day on the water.
