About the farm

A working farm sanctuary in the open country outside Sisters, with mountain views few vacation rentals can offer. Guests can relax under the stars, ride the farm trails on the complimentary bikes, and meet the sanctuary’s residents (visiting details to confirm).

The Farmhouse has two bedrooms, a hot tub, and welcomes dogs. The Cottage and Bunk House add room for more guests.
